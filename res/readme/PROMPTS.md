# README artwork

`connected-hero.webp` is a brand concept illustration generated with the built-in imagegen tool. It illustrates Mac–Android content handoff; abstract device screens are not application screenshots. The source PNG is retained as `connected-hero.png`; the published WebP uses quality 88. `Logo.png` was supplied as a brand reference. Real captures remain in `../screenshots/`.

## Final hero prompt

Use case: ads-marketing.
Asset type: premium wide hero illustration for the GitHub README of Clipy, a native macOS clipboard app that syncs with Android.
Input image 1: brand identity reference only, the existing Clipy blue rounded-square icon with two linked white/light-blue paper sheets. Keep the recognizable mark and cobalt blue.
Create one finished editorial 3D illustration, wide landscape approximately 1792x768 (or nearest supported wide landscape). A striking but refined product campaign, exceptionally polished studio art direction, abundant negative space and confident composition.
Scene: seamless very pale blue/ivory studio background. An elegant silver open laptop on the left, an upright slim Android-style phone with a small centered circular punch-hole camera on the right (no iPhone notch). Three softly rounded ivory paper cards arc diagonally through the open space between the devices: one has a simple text-line glyph, one a blue mountain/photo glyph, one a link glyph. A fluid translucent cobalt-blue ribbon gently connects the two devices behind the floating cards, suggesting local device-to-device movement. The ribbon is sculptural, restrained, not a tangled loop. Device screens are abstract deep cobalt fields with the Clipy linked-sheet mark; they are clearly illustrative, no fabricated app interfaces.
Layout: laptop spans left 45%, phone around right 22%, floating cards give center visual focus, artwork occupies central 80% with clean 7% margins. Laptop and phone grounded on same surface, convincing soft contact shadows, three-quarter perspective, balanced visual mass. Camera slightly above desk height. Soft daylight, subtly luminous edge light, satin aluminum, tactile matte paper and translucent blue glass, editorial product photography meets refined 3D paper sculpture. Palette electric Clipy blue, ice blue, warm ivory, restrained charcoal device edges. Bold blue surfaces provide contrast on GitHub white or dark backgrounds.
No text, no letters, no numbers, no headlines, no platform logos, no extra devices, no clouds, no stars, no watermark, no outer border. This is a brand concept illustration, not a screenshot. Professional finished marketing artwork, crisp edges, beautiful spatial hierarchy, not generic stock art.
